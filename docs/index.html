Privacy Policy — حصيلتي (Hasila)

Effective date: 2026-08-01 Developer: Angelos Onsy

1. What the app is

Hasila (حصيلتي) is a personal English vocabulary learning app that uses spaced-repetition to help you review words and situational phrases at the right time.

2. Data collected

Hasila itself collects no personal data and has no accounts or servers. Your learning data stays on your device (section 3). The only exception is the ads shown by Google AdMob (section 4) - rewarded video ads you choose to watch, and a small banner shown on the Settings and Add-word screens - which involve data that Google collects.

3. Where your data lives

Everything you enter in the app — vocabulary, review history, settings, streak — is stored only on your device, in the app's private local storage (Android localStorage via the Capacitor WebView). The app never uploads it. If you use "Export backup", the file is created on your device and handed to the share sheet you choose; where it goes after that is up to you.

4. Internet access and advertising

The app requests INTERNET permission so that ads can be loaded and shown through Google AdMob: optional rewarded video ads (for example, to unlock an extra daily review session or earn a streak freeze - shown only when you tap the button to watch one) and a small banner ad shown on the Settings and Add-word screens (never during a review session). Google AdMob may collect and use the advertising ID and related device data to serve and measure ads, under Google's own policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy . You can reset or opt out of personalised ads in Android Settings → Google → Ads. Fonts are bundled inside the app; no font requests are made.

5. Notifications

If you enable the daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification using the Android OS notification scheduler. No notification data is sent to any server. You can disable reminders at any time inside the app's Settings, or revoke the POST_NOTIFICATIONS permission in your Android system settings.

7. Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app is not directed to children. It shows ads (rewarded + banner, via Google AdMob) and has no social features.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be available in the app's Google Play listing and in this file (PRIVACY_POLICY.md) in the source repository. The effective date at the top will be updated.
